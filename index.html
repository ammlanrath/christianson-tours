06 / Preparation

Plan Your Day Trip

What to Bring

Comfortable walking shoes, sunglasses, SPF sunscreen, a government-issued photo ID (required for entry check points), and your camera or smartphone.

Strip Pickup Logistics

You will receive a confirmation SMS and email 24 hours prior with your exact tour lobby door number and the driver’s contact phone number.

Weather & Seasonal Tips

The West Rim sits at 4,000+ ft elevation and is typically 10–12°F cooler than Las Vegas. Bringing a light layer during spring and autumn is recommended.
